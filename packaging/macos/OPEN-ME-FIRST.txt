FeetBrowser for macOS
=====================

Drag FeetBrowser.app onto the Applications folder beside it, then read the
next paragraph before you double-click it, because the first launch will be
refused and the way round it is not where you would look.

The first launch is blocked. This is expected.
---------------------------------------------

Double-clicking gives you:

    "FeetBrowser" cannot be opened because Apple cannot check it for
    malicious software.

FeetBrowser is not signed with an Apple Developer ID and is not notarised.
That is a deliberate choice, not a mistake or a sign that something is
wrong with your download -- signing requires a paid Apple developer
membership, and this is a free project that would rather tell you the truth
than charge for it.

Gatekeeper is doing its job. It cannot tell "nobody paid Apple" apart from
"this is dangerous", so it says the scarier of the two.

How to open it anyway
---------------------

1. Double-click FeetBrowser and let it be refused. Click Done.

2. Open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, and scroll to the
   bottom. FeetBrowser is named there, with an "Open Anyway" button next
   to it.

3. Click Open Anyway, confirm, and enter your password.

That is it. macOS remembers your decision for this one application, and
every launch after this is an ordinary double-click.

If you have used a Mac for a while you may remember right-clicking an app
and choosing Open. That shortcut was removed in macOS 15 Sequoia, so on any
recent Mac it will not work for the first launch. Use System Settings.

If you prefer the Terminal, this is the same decision in one line:

    xattr -d com.apple.quarantine /Applications/FeetBrowser.app

Please do not turn Gatekeeper off entirely (spctl --master-disable). That
switches the check off for everything you will ever download, to solve a
problem with one file. FeetBrowser is not worth that, and neither is any
other app.

What you can check instead of a signature
-----------------------------------------

A signature would tell you who built this. Without one, here is what you
can verify yourself:

* Every build is made in public, by GitHub Actions, from the source in the
  repository. The log for the run that produced this file is public and
  shows every command.

* The app is self-contained and talks to nothing on your machine outside
  itself. To see that for yourself:

      codesign -dv /Applications/FeetBrowser.app
      otool -L /Applications/FeetBrowser.app/Contents/MacOS/FeetBrowser

  The second lists the system libraries it loads -- everything else it
  needs lives inside the bundle.

* Nothing is installed outside the app. There is no background service, no
  login item and no system extension. Uninstalling is dragging
  FeetBrowser.app to the Trash.
